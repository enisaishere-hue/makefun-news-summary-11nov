World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026. Russia and Ukraine agreed the largest prisoner swap of the war as two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended with no breakthrough; US envoy Jared Kushner said it would be the biggest humanitarian release yet, with numbers to be finalised the next day (AP, Reuters). At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres told leaders the climate crisis is outrunning us, with 2025 emissions at record highs (Reuters, UN News). The US charged three alleged Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 plots in Europe and the US, including one at RAF Fairford air base in England (US Justice Dept, BBC). The House voted 211-204 to send impeachment articles against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively blocking an immediate vote (Reuters). Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases and 5,387 deaths, the second largest on record (AP). NATO jets scrambled twice overnight after suspected drones triggered air alerts across the Baltic states; Vilnius and Kaunas airports closed briefly (Reuters). A US Army soldier was arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying for Russia (Met Police, CNN). Israel swore in a new Knesset with no bloc holding a majority, and President Herzog asked Gadi Eisenkot to form a government (Reuters). In Gaza, Israeli strikes killed three people, medics said (Reuters). Reuters found a blacklisted PLA-linked Chinese university bought two Nvidia H200 chips through a domestic supplier; Baird cut Nvidia to Neutral, slashing its target from $500 to $200 (Reuters). US October inflation rose 0.4%, hotter than forecast, ahead of the Fed's December meeting (Bureau of Labor Statistics). Bulgaria held a day of mourning after fog pile-ups on the Trakia motorway killed at least 11 (BTA). The Privy Council struck down Trinidad and Tobago's ban on consensual same-sex relations (BBC). 11 November is also Veterans Day in the US and Remembrance Day in the Commonwealth, marking the 1918 Armistice.
